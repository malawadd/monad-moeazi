# Cycle 04 — Calibration, Evidence Integrity, and Authority Explainability

## Research question

How can Unimoazi abstain under measured uncertainty, resist correlated/manipulated
evidence, and explain effective authority without creating new model or policy power?

## Candidate results

| Candidate | Result | Score | Audit conclusion |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| Calibrated selective prediction/abstention | Approved, Evaluation/Shadow only | 88 | Production proposal eligibility waits for market/horizon-specific empirical coverage and selective-risk evidence |
| Manipulation-aware evidence integrity | Approved with prerequisites | 92 | Extend the Evidence Event Fabric and claim ledger; provenance and signatures do not prove truth |
| Effective Authority Decision | Approved projection | 93 | Consume canonical gate/receipt owners; never become authority or a second policy engine |

## Evidence boundaries

- Adaptive conformal methods support long-run/marginal calibration research under
  shift, not individual-trade, per-regime, or PnL guarantees.
- W3C PROV and C2PA support lineage/content-origin assertions, not factual truth.
- The SEC social-account compromise is a concrete warning that official-channel
  identity alone cannot authorize action.
- OPA, NIST AI RMF, and Zanzibar are governance/authorization analogies, not trading
  implementations or compliance certification.

## Corrected hypotheses

- Selective prediction: uncertainty-receipt coverage can be a 100% invariant after
  cutover; false-positive and calibration percentages remain hypotheses and must be
  reported with abstention opportunity cost.
- Integrity: lineage/cluster coverage can be a 100% invariant; attack detection,
  quarantine precision, and authority reached by missed attacks are separate metrics.
- Authority projection: explanation coverage is an invariant only when every owner
  receipt is complete/fresh. Support-loop reduction requires an ambiguity baseline.
