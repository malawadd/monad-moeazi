# Cycle 10 — Artifact Semantics, Drift, and Collateral Exit

## Research question

After Cycle 09 establishes effective capabilities and venue-native risk inputs, what
must be proven about exact prepared bytes, executable dependencies and stressed
collateral before Unimoazi can safely certify execution?

## Approved mechanisms

### Artifact certification and drift

Resolve direct targets, proxies, implementations, beacons, admins, domains, hook/pool
configuration and adapter/SDK/image dependencies. Mandatory boundary reads before
preparation and signing complement event ingestion. Unknown, expired or drifted state
blocks new exposure and invalidates dependent routes, grants and equivalence receipts.

### Intent-to-bytes equivalence

Freeze the already approved envelope, recursively decode exact transaction or typed
message bytes, simulate at a pinned context, classify state deltas and require exact
hash parity through Particle/Magic signing and dispatch. Unknown selectors, recipients,
subplans or effects block. The receipt is evidence, never reusable authorization.

### Collateral and exit capacity

Map exact collateral dependencies and run versioned depeg/outage/depth/withdrawal
scenarios. New exposure requires both stressed maintenance coverage and executable
close capacity. Withdrawal-now, queued and unavailable are distinct; no automatic
bridge, swap, close or withdrawal is introduced.

## Evidence and limitations

Protocol standards and production documentation establish observable commands,
proxy/config identities, collateral and withdrawal mechanisms. They do not prove code
safety, semantic decoder correctness, issuer solvency, future simulation fidelity or
economic benefit. All percentage ranges remain mutation/fault/stress hypotheses.

## Changes from earlier cycles

The equivalence receipt does not revive the held second `ExecutionIntent`. Artifact
certification does not duplicate capability state or general build provenance. The
collateral envelope nests into risk/sizing/preflight and remains separate from Cycle
09's venue-native liquidation receipt.
