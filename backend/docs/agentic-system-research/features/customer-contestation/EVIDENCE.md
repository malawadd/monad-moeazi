submission, response, action and disposition patterns. They are process analogies;
their legal applicability to Unimoazi is not asserted. Repository review found no
customer case/appeal/remedy owner; operational incidents and internal disputed evidence
answer different questions.

Limitations: complaints can be false or incomplete; human review can be inconsistent;
case data is privacy-sensitive; legal deadlines/applicability need counsel; a finding
cannot reverse immutable external effects. Sources and exact links are registered in
[SOURCES.md](../../SOURCES.md).

No complaint-rule compliance, independent appeal, restitution, fairness or customer-
satisfaction claim is made without qualified owners and evidence.

The local implementation exercises only deterministic synthetic workflow invariants;
it does not change the evidence classification, establish legal applicability, or
demonstrate a staffed customer-remedy outcome.
