# Validation and Promotion

Test duplicate/replay, wrong account, invalid representative, malicious/PII attachment,
correction during investigation, disputed immutable receipt, reviewer conflict/self-
appeal, qualification expiry, delivery failure, boundary-time appeal, wrong remedy
asset/account/chain, uncertain remedy and premature closure.

Thresholds: 100% authenticated scope/policy binding; zero truth overwrite, model
finding, cross-tenant access or automatic remedy; 100% findings cite evidence/reviewer;
100% financial remedies have separate valid authorization/receipt; zero closed cases
with pending appeal/remedy; deterministic allowed transitions.

Promote only after staffing/policy gates and synthetic exercises pass. Kill for model
adjudication, complaint-as-truth, history rewrite, automatic money/trade, fake appeal
independence, unsupported legal claims, default publication, unresolved closure or case
submission becoming authority control.

## Baseline, datasets, and sample requirements

The baseline is the current repository behavior or the same frozen plan with this
feature disabled. Datasets begin with versioned local fixtures, adversarial cases and
chronological replay that satisfies Evidence Lifecycle and Usage Rights. Deterministic
safety invariants are checked on every enumerated case, not a statistical sample. Any
percentage or quality hypothesis requires a preregistered power analysis and held-out
chronological cohort; an underpowered result is inconclusive. Promotion requires every
feature-specific threshold plus replayability and cost/latency bounds. One hard-gate or
safety-invariant breach triggers kill/demotion and preserved evidence.

## Local fixture verification

The local corpus has 96 checked-in scenarios, independently stored expectations,
deterministic replay and adversarial lifecycle/evidence/reviewer/appeal/remedy cases.
Its result is not evidence of legal applicability, customer outcomes, delivery, remedy,
or product readiness.
