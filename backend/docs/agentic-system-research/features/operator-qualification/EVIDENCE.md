## Limitations

- Drills can teach to the test and may not predict real behavior.
- Reviewer disagreement and small staffing pools undermine reliability.
- Qualification adds human latency and personnel-data obligations.
- System/playbook changes can invalidate prior readiness quickly.
- Passing is not authorization or a safety guarantee.

No professional certification, employment assessment, incident reduction or operator
competence guarantee is claimed.

## Local implementation evidence

Measured evidence is limited to deterministic replay of checked-in synthetic artifacts:
strict parsing, role/version closure, drill oracle, independent review, expiry/revocation
folds, and non-authoritative consumption receipts. It does not measure real staffing,
human competence, incident response, or a production outcome.
