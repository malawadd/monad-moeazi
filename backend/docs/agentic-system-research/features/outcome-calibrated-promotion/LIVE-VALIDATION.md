# Live validation gate

Product activation remains blocked. It requires remediated and revalidated
authoritative Feature 33 execution/TCA and Feature 35 outcome owners, exact product
configuration and activation ownership, explicit rollback authorization, data rights
and retention review, powered preregistered chronological cohorts, independent
statistical/security/human review, fault-injected rollback SLO, durable post-
activation Shadow monitoring, manual baselines, abort/cleanup/evidence retention, and
separate approved costs and activation for the named product mutation. Automatic
promotion is out of scope. Automatic demotion requires a later separately approved
product contract and never changes customer authority.
