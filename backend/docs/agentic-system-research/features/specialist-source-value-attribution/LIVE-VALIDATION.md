reviewed honest live citation contract, authoritative provenance and claim identity,
qualified contract owners and usage-rights approval. It also requires at least 100
mature chronological pairs per role/market/regime/horizon stratum, power analysis,
pre-registered objectives, explicit provider-cost approval, finite replay then Shadow
non-inferiority, drift monitoring, rollback and a separate approval before any product
role or source gate. Cost is unknown until that future experiment is scoped.
