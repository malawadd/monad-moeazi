# Commercial and approval checklist

No application was submitted and no commercial commitment was made.

For Transak, MoonPay and Banxa ask about:

- Developer/sandbox access and partner eligibility for a beginner-facing trading app.
- Confirmed canonical USDC on Arbitrum delivery and current supported payment methods.
- Integration/setup/minimum-volume fees and whether an integration waiver exists.
- Partner commission configuration (Moeazi starts at zero) and checkout fee disclosure.
- Refunds, chargebacks, purchase support ownership and outage escalation.
- Custom Tabs/referrer/IP/return-domain and signed-webhook requirements.
- Co-marketing, launch support, accelerator benefits or negotiated credits.

For bundler/paymaster partners ask about:

- Kernel 0.3.3, EIP-7702 and EntryPoint v0.7 support without changing the owner address.
- Enforceable owner/selector/network/gas policy restrictions, spend ceilings and rollback.
- Pending-operation lookup/recovery after retirement and provider rate limits.
- Startup/gas-grant eligibility, grant expiry and post-grant pricing.
- API overage and incident response; distinguish sponsorship from free gas.

[Pimlico startup support](https://docs.pimlico.io/guides/why-pimlico/startups) advertises
applications, not guaranteed awards. Its [published pricing](https://docs.pimlico.io/guides/pricing)
includes actual mainnet gas plus a 10% surcharge and possible API overage. Other
commercial terms remain unknown until the provider agrees them.

Before any billable setup, provider probe, deployment or canary: identify the service,
state expected usage/cost (or explicitly unknown), and obtain approval for that action.
A canary must separately authorize any gas expenditure. Do not register, submit
grant applications, add billing cards or fund provider accounts automatically.
