## Coverage rules

- A source audit proves that a route is declared; a screenshot proves one
  viewport/state. Neither proves the complete tap path or that Back, keyboard,
  signer, screen reader and failure branches work.
- Historical V1 reducer/view fixtures are useful transition evidence only. The
  critique in the four V1 feedback images supersedes any broad V1 completion
  claim. See `validation.md` for the versioned record.
- The four newer V3 screenshots call out routine alarm copy, hidden funding
  action, below-fold placement and manual deposit entry at zero balance. The
  V4 captures refine top-up placement: initial setup only on Home; repeat
  actions at the bottom of Settings beside Receive/Send.
- V2 has no visual pass recorded here. The supervisor reports that the browser
  inspection attempt was blocked by the CUA URL policy; no screenshot or
  interaction result is inferred from that attempt.
- Human sessions are still pending; this matrix records intended coverage, not
  participant outcomes.
